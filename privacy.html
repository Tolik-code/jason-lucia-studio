Privacy Policy

Jason & Lucia Studio · Effective date: 14 November 2026

Who uses this app

Jason & Lucia Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “Jason & Lucia” (@jasonluciafan) to run that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to access the owner's own YouTube channel only:

  • videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publishing settings of the channel's own videos;
  • the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention and similar metrics).

The app does not access data of any other YouTube users or Google accounts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the channel's own videos and to analyse the channel's own performance. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are never published or shared. Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to analyse the channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
